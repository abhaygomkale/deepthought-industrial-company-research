# Federer ICP Research — Industrial Automation & Instrumentation Companies (Pune)

## Overview

This project is a research-driven B2B market intelligence study focused on identifying and evaluating high-potential industrial automation, instrumentation, electronics manufacturing, and Industry 4.0 companies in Pune, India.

The objective was not to create a generic company directory, but to build an intelligently qualified ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) list using a structured scoring framework called the **Federer Score**.

The research focuses on:

- Industrial Automation
- Process Instrumentation
- Industrial Electronics
- Industry 4.0 / IIoT
- PLC / HMI Manufacturers
- Sensors & Measurement Systems
- Mechatronics
- Industrial Metrology
- EMS / Electronics Manufacturing

---

# Project Objective

The goal of this project was to:

- Discover high-quality industrial technology companies in Pune
- Research founder and leadership backgrounds
- Evaluate technical depth and manufacturing capability
- Identify differentiated industrial product companies
- Analyze growth signals and market tailwinds
- Create a structured ICP scoring system for qualification

This project simulates how:

- B2B SaaS companies
- Venture firms
- Strategic sales teams
- Market intelligence teams
- Consulting firms

research and qualify industrial companies before outreach or investment.

---

# Federer Scoring Framework

Each company was evaluated across 6 core criteria.

| Criteria | Weight |
|---|---|
| C1 — Manufacturer Strength | 10 |
| C2 — India-Based Operations | 5 |
| C3 — Differentiation / Product Depth | 25 |
| C4 — Technical Decision Maker Quality | 20 |
| C5 — Industry Tailwinds | 20 |
| C6 — Growth Signals | 20 |

Total Score = 100
